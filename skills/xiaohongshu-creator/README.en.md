# Xiaohongshu Hit Content Creator / xiaohongshu-creator

---

## Introduction

A single Skill covering the full Xiaohongshu (RED) content creation pipeline — note generation, copywriting rewrite, title generation & scoring, cover design, and prohibited-word detection — turning raw ideas into publish-ready content.

**Core Value**

- **5-in-1 creation toolkit**: notes, rewrites, titles, covers, and compliance checks in one place.
- **Data-driven**: built on a library of 2,000+ daily-collected hit notes, so creation is grounded in real trends.
- **Publish-ready output**: finished content you can post directly, no extra editing needed.

**Who It's For**

- 📝 **Xiaohongshu creators** — end-to-end creation from topic to publish, no more writer's block.
- 🛍️ **Brand / e-commerce teams** — fast product-copy rewrites and batch content production.
- 🏢 **MCN / content planners** — differentiated content at scale for creator networks.

---

## Features

### Core Features

- **Note generation**: search hit notes in your niche, extract traffic patterns, and generate a complete note with title, body, and tags.
- **Copywriting rewrite**: convert any material into Xiaohongshu style with title, emoji, and tags in a natural, conversational tone.
- **Title generation**: produce 10 hit-style titles, each with a match score, reference hits, and recommendation rationale.
- **Title scoring**: six-dimension weighted scoring (S/A/B/C grade) with specific optimization suggestions and revised versions.
- **Cover design**: AI analyzes visual patterns of hit covers in your niche and outputs 3 differentiated design plans with image-generation prompts.
- **Prohibited-word detection**: accepts text, files, images, or URLs — flags violations, suggests replacements, and produces a safe version.
